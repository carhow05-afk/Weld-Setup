SIP WELDMATE AUTOPLUS 160A

01 / Your welding job

Machine limits & safety

230 V / 16 A supply. Published output up to 160 A; duty cycle 30% at 160 A at 20°C. Follow the rating plate and manual. Published mild steel range 0.8–5.5 mm is not a guarantee for every process or joint.

Use suitable helmet, gloves, protective clothing, fume extraction and fire precautions. Secure cylinders upright. Switch off and isolate before changing connections. Never weld closed containers. Do not use these estimates for safety-critical welds.
